# Known recurring patterns

A large share of tickets are the same handful of underlying mechanisms wearing different campaign names. Matching a ticket's symptoms to one of these doesn't mean skip verification — it means you already know exactly what to check to confirm or rule it out for this specific player.

## Settlement-after-window-close (the single most common pattern)

**The shape:** a mission/campaign requires a cumulative stake, bet count, or wagering total, with `settled: true` required for each contributing bet. A player places their qualifying bet(s) well within the window, but the bet's *event* (the match, the spin round) doesn't finish and settle until after the window has already closed. The window-close process only sees what's settled at that instant — a bet accepted on time but settled minutes late never gets counted, and the instance freezes at an incomplete state forever (it doesn't get a later update once the bet actually settles, because the instance already closed).

**How to recognize it in the data:** on the closing instance's `customer_campaign_status` record, look for a gap between `no_of_bets_made` (or `currency_amount_of_bets_made`) and `no_of_settled_bets_made` (`currency_amount_of_settled_bets_made`) — e.g. `no_of_bets_made: 10` but `no_of_settled_bets_made: 9`. The missing bet(s) sit in the `eligible` (unsettled) array with an `accept_time` shortly before the window's close timestamp. `status`/`display_status` stays `in_progress`/`pending`, never flips to `qualified`/`complete`.

**A UI trap that makes this worse:** the backoffice/CS-facing UI sometimes surfaces the raw `qualification.progress` percentage directly, which can show 100% while the authoritative `status` never completed — a ticket saying "Betler shows 100%" is not proof the mission actually finished. Always check `no_of_bets_made` vs. `no_of_settled_bets_made` (or the currency equivalents) and the real `status` field, not just the percentage.

**Confirmed recurring on specific campaign families** (campaign IDs will drift as new generations launch, but the mechanism is the same): the "Road to 10 Mission" / "Road to 10 Super Mission" weekly missions, their daily ACCA-shaped sibling, the "Accumulator Bet Mission," and the Spooky Jack Clover Chance weekly cashback. Confirmed on wagering-bonus conversion too, not just mission windows — a bonus's `wagered` can exactly match its `wager_limit` and it can still show `state: "expired"` because the final qualifying bet's settlement landed right at the bonus's own expiry timestamp and lost the race to flip to `complete`.

**How to apply:** when you see this signature, don't treat it as inconclusive or as player error — the player met the real-world requirement (placing qualifying bets in time); the engine's own window-close timing is what's at fault. Recommend a GWG award directly. If this is the Nth confirmed case on the same campaign family within a short window, say so explicitly when tagging — a wave of these on one rollover is worth flagging to Product as a systemic gap (no settlement grace period before finalizing an instance), not re-diagnosed from scratch each time.

**Don't over-apply it, though:** always confirm the specific signature (a genuine settled/unsettled gap right at the close timestamp) rather than assuming it from "player says mission was complete." A player can also simply be short by one bet with *nothing* pending (no unsettled bets at all, progress genuinely under 100%) — that's a plain shortfall, not this bug. Check the actual numbers every time.

## Campaign contention — an earlier-opted-in campaign consumed the event first

Betler evaluates a customer's non-mission campaigns in ascending opt-in-time order; the first campaign whose rule matches an event consumes it — later-opted campaigns never see that event, even if their own rule would also match. (Missions are exempt — they see every event regardless of other campaigns.)

**Signature:** the player insists they met a requirement, but the campaign shows genuinely **0% progress** (not "close but short" — that's a different thing). This is the tell that contention, not a genuine shortfall, is worth checking.

**How to confirm it's real contention, not just "same vertical":** pull the player's other campaigns via `customer_campaign_status`, sorted by `opt_in_out_date` ascending, looking for one opted into earlier. A candidate is only a real threat if **both**:
1. Its `qualificationType` actually accumulates over events (`"standard"`, `"onCampaignEnd"`, etc.) — **not** `"automatic"`/`noOptInAndQualification`. A one-shot auto-qualifier (`erlang_expression: "true"`, flips to qualified instantly) poses no ongoing threat no matter how broad its rule looks, *with one confirmed exception below*.
2. It has genuinely similar qualifying conditions to the campaign under investigation — not just "same vertical." Both need cumulative slot spins, or both need the same specific game/provider, etc.

**Confirmed recurring exception — check this pair by name, don't re-derive:** campaign `b2adac5f-e1f8-4134-9c50-3b6b1e16bda5` ("Deposit & Get," `noOptInAndQualification`/automatic, deposit ≥€25 → flat €10 Free Bet, monthly, always-on) repeatedly consumes a player's deposit before an opted-in Churn15 reload offer (`805257cb-14fb-460f-9b1f-8e75e141dcf1`, "50% Reload Bonus up to €100," deposit ≥€10) ever evaluates it — confirmed across multiple separate players, always with the `b2adac5f` bonus landing at the exact same second as the deposit. The moment `b2adac5f` shows up as "qualified" on a ticket about a *different* deposit/reload campaign, check for `805257cb` specifically — this is a known, standing contention pair despite `b2adac5f` technically being "automatic." A useful fast query for any deposit-type campaign: `opensearch_search` scoped to `qualification_progress`/`qualification.deposits`/`campaign.*` fields keeps the payload small enough to pull a player's *full* campaign history in one call and grep for any record with `no_of_deposits_required` set.

**How to apply:** this is a genuine platform-side issue (two offers that shouldn't be allowed to compete for the same audience), not player error. Recommend a manual award for the blocked campaign.

**Lookalike, not actually contention — "first qualifying bet" resolved by settlement order:** a "losing bets only, first qualifying bet" mechanic can lock onto whichever of two nearly-simultaneous bets *settled* first, not whichever was *placed* first. Before escalating an odd-looking "wrong bet locked in" case as a bug, pull both bets' `accept_time` and `settle_time` and check whether settlement order (not placement order) explains it — that may be the intended behavior, not a miss.

## Display-rounding: "100% completed" shown with a sub-threshold amount

Confirmed on a weekly cashback campaign: the player's own in-app screenshot showed the progress bar and a "100% completed" label, while the number directly above read something like €49.90/€50.00 — a direct on-screen contradiction caused by the front-end rounding the percentage up before the real cumulative total reaches the threshold. Backend data matched the sub-threshold number; the player was genuinely not yet qualified.

**How to apply:** when multiple same-day tickets land on one campaign all stuck just under 100%, check (or ask for) the player's own screenshot. If it shows "100%/completed" text alongside a sub-threshold euro amount in the same image, that's this display bug, not a crediting failure — no award (genuinely short), but worth flagging the rounding bug itself to product/front-end. This is a *different* sub-pattern from a paused campaign instance that's silently stopped incorporating new qualifying play at all (check `gaming_session_ticker` for qualifying activity timestamped *after* the campaign status doc's own `updated_at` — if real post-freeze activity alone closes the gap, that's the stuck-instance bug, recommend award).

## Missing event in a whitelist

A single omitted `event_id` in a campaign's event whitelist is invisible from any one ticket in isolation — it looks identical to a market/stake/odds miss. It only becomes visible once you compare multiple players' failing-but-otherwise-qualifying bets against each other and notice they cluster on one specific event. If several tickets on the same campaign all show "met every requirement, still didn't count," trace each bet's actual `event_id`/`market_id`/odds/stake via `customer_events` and compare side by side rather than evaluating each ticket alone.

If confirmed as a real operational gap (the event was genuinely within the promo's advertised scope but missing from configuration), this is usually resolved by a bulk payout to everyone affected, posted in-channel — not individual manual awards per ticket. Once that resolution is announced for a specific campaign/event, apply it directly to any further ticket hitting the same gap rather than re-investigating each one from scratch; still verify that the specific player's bet actually lands on the confirmed-missing event before applying it.

## Free Spins "game not available" / bonus won't launch

Never swap a Free Spins bonus to a different game — it's deliberately scoped to the one game named in the offer. Standard procedure:
1. Have the player try the other client (app↔browser) or clear cache/cookies and retry — most "game not available" symptoms are a stale client-side cache issue.
2. If that doesn't fix it: re-credit the **same bonus, same game** — not a substitute.
3. If it looks like a genuine platform-wide outage (not just this player), escalate to product support.

A related, distinct symptom: the FS bonus is valid and unexpired in `bondet_state`, but the game client loads in normal real-money mode instead of auto-launching bonus mode — not a declined popup, not expired, not "game not available." Same remedy (re-credit same bonus). If this exact symptom recurs across multiple players on the same FS-drop campaign within a short window, it's a confirmed systemic bug on that specific bonus/campaign, not isolated incidents.

## Rejected spin carrying a real, uncredited win

`fsinfo_state`'s `used`/`remaining` counters only reflect spins that were *successfully credited* — they are not proof a bonus was never played. A `gaming_ticker` round with `status: "rejected"` can still carry a real calculated win in `credits[].amount` that never reached the wallet-crediting pipeline at all (no delta in `fsinfo_updates` either). If a player disputes an FS outcome, don't stop at `fsinfo_state` looking untouched — check `gaming_ticker` for a rejected round with a nonzero win in the same window. If a fresh re-grant of the same bonus reproduces the identical rejection, that's a confirmed crediting-pipeline bug (escalate to game-integrations), and the fix is crediting the specific calculated win amount, not just re-granting the bonus again.

## Cashback mechanics — read the category/provider/stake gates, and the direction of the number

- **"Lose & Get" / internal names containing "L&G" or similar accumulator-cup-style names can be loss-contingent cashback, not a flat freebet**, even when an internal name contains "FB." Check `dynamicBonusInput` on the live campaign definition before recommending anything — `cashbackWageringLossesOnBets`/`cashbackNetLossesOnSpins` mean the qualifying bet/session must have **lost** to trigger payout; `wageringLosses: 0` or a net-win result is correctly €0, not a missing award.
- **Category and provider are two independent gates** on a gaming cashback campaign's `erlang_expression` — a campaign can restrict on `category(Attr)` (e.g. slot/dice_slot only, table games and live casino usually excluded), on `provider(Attr)`/`game_id(Attr)` (a specific provider/game list), on both, or neither. Read the full expression every time; don't assume only one axis is checked.
- **There is often a separate minimum cumulative stake to even trigger evaluation** (`minSumGamingBetsOnCampaignEnd` or similar) — a real, large net loss still correctly shows 0%/`in_progress` if the player's qualifying-category stake falls under that minimum.
- **Compute net loss independently from `gaming_session_ticker`, filtered to the qualifying category, over the window from `max(player's own opt_in_out_date, campaign.dates.start)` through `campaign.dates.finish`** — not the raw campaign start. `customer_attributes`' aggregate profit/loss fields cover a much wider window than a specific campaign round and are not reliable confirmation either way; they've been directly contradicted by the actual campaign-window computation. Never trust a signed `netLosses`/similar field's sign at face value without this independent check — it has been directly contradicted by manual player-activity verification before, in a case where it read as a net win when the player had actually lost.
- **A player can hold both a CORE cashback campaign and a sibling MV/HV tier-variant campaign for the same round** — if CORE shows 0%/tombstoned, check whether the tier variant actually did the calculating (same dates, different campaign_id) before treating CORE's emptiness as an anomaly. This is the contention pattern above in a tier-variant flavor, not a separate bug.

## F2P/Hunch/Honey boundary — Super Wheel, Super Predictor, Serial Winner/Super Streak

Qualifying in Betler for one of these campaigns (`b7200814`/"Super Wheel" and siblings with `additionalBonusConfiguration` mapping to a "Honey Ticket"/"Honey Token") grants **eligibility to play an external F2P game** — it does not itself entitle a bonus. The actual reward only materializes if the player plays and wins there, via a separate Superology→Betler award bridge. "Qualified in Betler + nothing in `bondet_state`" is consistent with three very different situations (never played yet; played and lost; played, won, but the award bridge failed) that are **not distinguishable from Betler data alone** — the per-ticket Honey Ticket status/award data lives entirely outside any Kafka/OpenSearch topic reachable from Betler tooling (it's a live HTTP call to Honey's own backend, a different company's infrastructure).

**Do this, in order:**
1. **Always check first whether the resulting bonus already landed as an ordinary Betler bonus** — search `bondet_state` by `customer_id`, scoped to the campaign's date window (filter by `game_id` if the ticket names a game). Many of these campaigns resolve into a normal FS/bonus record just like any other reward, especially when `additionalBonusConfiguration` names an actual bonus rather than a bare "Honey Ticket" token — this is often found directly and completely, with no need to invoke the Honey-backoffice limitation at all.
2. Only if that search is genuinely empty: confirm eligibility (`customer_campaign_status` shows `qualified`/`opted_in`) and state plainly that visibility stops there — ask for a Betler Backoffice → Player → Bonuses → Honey Tickets screenshot showing the specific ticket's status and its **per-ticket `Bonus ID`** (a different, more specific ID than the segment-level bonus template ID).
3. If that screenshot shows status "Used" + a specific `Bonus ID`: check `bondet_state` for that **exact** ID. Absent there = a confirmed bug, safe to recommend a manual award for that value. "Issued" (not used) = player hasn't played yet, nothing owed.

## Casino App Offer eligibility gap

Multiple same-day tickets claiming "downloaded the app, wagered, no FS, can't find the campaign" for the Casino App Offer family (Bet10Get50FS/Slotomoji and similar) have shown either a brand-new `eligible` record created only minutes before the check, or **zero** record at all even for long-tenure customers. This looks like either a targeting/segment gap or a lag between the qualifying in-app action and the eligibility record landing in Betler. Treat a third occurrence of this exact shape as a confirmed pattern, not three coincidences — flag to crm-ops-greece for eligibility-segment review rather than re-diagnosing each one as isolated.
